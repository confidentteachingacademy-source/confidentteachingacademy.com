Introducing

The CALM Method

A four-part classroom routine system that creates structure, reduces stress, and builds quiet authority — without asking you to become a different person.

C

Clear Starts

Get the first five minutes right, every single time. Behaviour becomes easier to manage throughout the entire lesson.

A

Arrive Prepared

Most classroom problems are preventable before the lesson even begins. Preparation removes the need for confrontation.

L

Lead Key Students

Control the few, and the many follow. You don't need to manage every student equally.

M

Maintain Consistency

Consistency builds respect. Intensity does not. Your standards stay the same, even when your emotions change.
