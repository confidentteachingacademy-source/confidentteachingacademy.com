Introducing the CALM Method

Four strategies ordered from easiest to implement today to most challenging to sustain over time. Start with C. Build from there.

C
Claim the Start

The first five minutes of every lesson are where your classroom is won or lost. Own them deliberately, every single day.

A
Arrive Prepared

Preparation goes beyond lesson planning. Know which students need your attention before they walk through the door.

L
Leverage Key Relationships

Your ability to build genuine relationships with students and parents is not a weakness. It is your most powerful classroom management tool.

M
Mean It Every Time

Students do not respect the strictest teacher. They respect the most predictable one. Mean what you say. Every time.
